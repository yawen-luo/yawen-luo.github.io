About Me

👋 Hi, I am Yawen Luo. I am an undergraduate student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, studying Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) with a minor in Industrial Design (ID). My interests lie in Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), Extended Reality (XR), Human-Centered AI, and intelligent interactive systems.

My current focus is adaptive XR interaction with multimodal sensing and AI.

I am particularly interested in systems that can understand human behavior and context, adapt to users, and support meaningful interaction in real-world settings. My recent work explores eye tracking, VR interaction, and LLM-supported dialogue.

My engineering background gives me tools to build technical systems, while design training pushes me to ask who those systems are for, how people experience them, and what makes a technology worth keeping in everyday life.
