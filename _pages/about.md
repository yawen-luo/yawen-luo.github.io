I am an undergraduate student at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, studying **Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)** with a minor in **Industrial Design (ID)**.

My current interests lie at the intersection of **human-computer interaction, extended reality, human-centered AI, and intelligent interactive systems**. I am particularly interested in building computational systems that connect technical capability with human needs, and in exploring how interactive technologies can become more adaptive, accessible, and meaningful in real-world contexts.

## Research Interests

- Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
- Extended Reality (XR) and immersive interaction
- Human-Centered AI
- Multimodal and intelligent interactive systems
- Accessible and inclusive technology

## Selected Research

### Physical Understanding in AI-Generated Video
**Research Project · Nov. 2025 – Jan. 2026**

Investigated how state-of-the-art AI video generation models follow physical laws. I contributed to the research framework by developing evaluation cases and judgment criteria for model behavior.

### LabVIEW-Based Physical Sandbox Control System for Disaster Relief Simulation
**Engineering Research Project · May 2026 – Aug. 2026**

Developed core LabVIEW modules for control, monitoring, UDP communication, and system integration in an energy management system involving LabVIEW, Simulink, and Raspberry Pi.

## Selected Projects

My design and engineering projects explore how technology can support people in everyday, social, and health-related contexts. Current and past work includes **Smell Walk**, **Anxinji**, an **AI-assisted public expression training system**, inclusive design projects, and **Pilly**, a family health companion robot.
