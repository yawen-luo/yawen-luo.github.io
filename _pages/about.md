## About Me

I am an undergraduate student at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, studying **Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)** with a minor in **Industrial Design (ID)**. My interests lie at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction, Extended Reality, Human-Centered AI, and intelligent interactive systems**.

I am particularly interested in building systems that connect technical capability with human needs: systems that can better understand context, support communication, and remain useful in real-world settings.
