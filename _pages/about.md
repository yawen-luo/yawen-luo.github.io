      <h2>About Me</h2>

      <p>Hi, I am <strong>Yawen Luo</strong>, an undergraduate student at <strong>Shanghai Jiao Tong University</strong>, studying <strong>Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)</strong> with a minor in <strong>Industrial Design (ID)</strong>.</p>

      <p>My interests lie at the intersection of <strong>Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)</strong>, <strong>Extended Reality (XR)</strong>, <strong>Human-Centered AI</strong>, and intelligent interactive systems. I am particularly interested in systems that understand human behavior and context, adapt to users, and support meaningful interaction in real-world settings.</p>

      <div class="yw-highlight">
        <p><strong>Current focus.</strong> I am exploring adaptive XR interaction using eye tracking, multimodal sensing, and LLM-supported dialogue, while continuing to build across both engineering and interaction-design contexts.</p>
      </div>

      <p>My engineering background gives me tools to build technical systems, while design training pushes me to ask who those systems are for, how people experience them, and what makes a technology worth keeping in everyday life.</p>
    </section>
