--- layout: archive title: "Projects" permalink: /projects/ author_profile: true --- My projects combine engineering, interaction design, and human-centered problem framing. This page will gradually expand into full project case studies with images, videos, prototypes, and design documentation. ## Featured Projects ### Smell Walk A web-based project for documenting olfactory experiences through writing. It began from an interest in smell as an often-overlooked sense with a strong connection to emotion and memory. ### Anxinji A health-recording product concept designed for older adults within a short-video platform ecosystem. The project helped me examine the trade-offs between product ideals, interaction flow, platform constraints, and user retention. ### AI-Assisted Public Expression Training System A system exploring how AI can support more constructive public expression and discussion. The project combines **RAG, multi-agent systems, and prompt engineering** with a broader question: how can tools help genuine dialogue happen? ### Inclusive Kitchenware & Age-Friendly Community Park Two inclusive-design explorations: kitchenware for visually impaired users and a rapid design challenge focused on intergenerational communication in public space. ### Pilly — AI Family Health Companion A non-humanoid desktop health companion based on the principle of **“presence without interruption”**: asynchronous interaction, low attention demand, and no unread-message pressure. ### TapTap Spotlight Game Development Competition A team game-development project completed in 2024, reflecting an earlier stage of my 3D modeling and interactive-development workflow.
