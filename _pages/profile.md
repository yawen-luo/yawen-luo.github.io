I study **Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)** with a minor in **Industrial Design (ID)** at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**.

Engineering gives me tools to understand and build systems; design makes me ask who those systems are for, how people experience them, and what makes a technology worth keeping in everyday life.

My current interests lie in **Human-Computer Interaction, Extended Reality, Human-Centered AI, and intelligent interactive systems**. Across research and independent projects, I often work between technical implementation, problem framing, interaction design, and communication.

Outside academic and project work, I enjoy street photography, unstructured travel, cooking, long-form conversations, and writing.
