I study **Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)** with a minor in **Industrial Design (ID)** at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**.

My academic path is intentionally interdisciplinary. Engineering gives me tools to understand and build technical systems; design pushes me to ask who those systems are for, how people experience them, and what makes a technology worth keeping in everyday life.

I am especially interested in **human-computer interaction, extended reality, human-centered AI, and intelligent interactive systems**. Across research and independent projects, I tend to work between technical implementation, problem framing, interaction design, and communication.

A long-term question behind much of my work is how technology can become more attentive to people without demanding more of their attention.

## Beyond Work

I enjoy street photography, slow and unstructured travel, cooking, long-form conversations, and writing. I am drawn to work that notices concrete people and overlooked experiences, and I hope to build things that remain useful and meaningful beyond a single moment or trend.
